A Demat (Dematerialized) Account works exactly like a bank locker, but instead of gold or cash, it holds your financial securities in digital format. In India, two depositories manage these accounts: NSDL (National Securities Depository Ltd) and CDSL (Central Depository Services Ltd).

Who is it for?

If you want to buy shares in the stock market (Equity Delivery), hold Mutual Funds in Demat form, or invest in Sovereign Gold Bonds, a Demat account is mandatory.

Fees & Charges

  • Account Opening Fee: Usually waived off (Free) by most modern brokers.
  • AMC (Annual Maintenance Charge): A yearly fee ranging from ₹300 to ₹800 to keep the account active.
  • DP Charges: A flat fee (approx ₹13.50 + GST) applied only when you sell shares (debit from account).

Brokerage Models

When choosing a Trading Account, understanding brokerage is key:

Type Example Best For
Discount Broker Zerodha, Groww, Upstox Self-directed traders. Flat fee (e.g., ₹20 per trade). Low cost.
Full Service ICICI Direct, HDFC Sec Beginners needing advisory/tips. Percentage fee (e.g., 0.55%). Higher cost.

Required Documents

  • PAN Card: Mandatory for all financial market transactions in India.
  • Income Proof: (Salary slip/ITR) Only required if you want to trade in Futures & Options (F&O).
  • Cancelled Cheque: To link your primary bank account for fund transfers.
