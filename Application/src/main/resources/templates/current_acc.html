Fee Schedule
The "Hidden" Costs

Transactions are unlimited — but not always free. Always read the Schedule of Charges carefully.

Cash Deposit Charges
Most common friction point. Banks give a free limit (e.g., ₹2 Lakhs or 10× MAB/month). Deposits beyond this incur a fee of approximately ₹3 per ₹1,000. If your business is cash-heavy, always compare this limit first.
Non-Maintenance (MAB) Penalty
If your balance falls below the Minimum Average Balance, the penalty is significantly steeper than savings accounts. These can range from ₹500 to ₹5,000+ per month depending on the shortfall and bank tier.
Cheque Return Charges
Penalties for bounced cheques are high to discourage bad credit behaviour. Inward cheque return can cost ₹350–750 per instance; outward return is typically ₹150–500. Multiple bounces can also affect your credit score.
Folio Charges
Some banks charge a nominal fee based on the number of ledger entries (folio pages) generated per quarter. This is increasingly rare with digital banking, but worth checking in the fee schedule for older account types.

Advanced Feature

The Sweep-In / Sweep-Out Facility

Current Accounts earn zero interest — which means idle money silently loses value against inflation. The Sweep-In solves this automatically.

Idle Balance
Cash above your set threshold (e.g. ₹1 Lakh) sits idle in your Current Account
Auto Sweep Out
Bank automatically moves surplus funds into a high-interest Fixed Deposit
Earn Interest
FD earns 5–7% p.a. while you retain on-demand liquidity
Auto Sweep In
When a cheque is written, the FD is broken automatically to fund it
Decision Guide
How to Choose the Right Bank

Don't just pick the nearest branch. These three factors will save you thousands in hidden fees.

01
Cash Deposit Limits

If your business is cash-heavy (retail, restaurants), choose the bank with the highest free cash deposit limit. Going over costs ₹3 per ₹1,000 — it adds up fast.

02
Digital Interface Quality

Corporate Net Banking interfaces vary wildly. Look for one supporting bulk salary payments and integration with Tally, Zoho, or your accounting software.

03
Overdraft Eligibility

Some banks offer unsecured overdrafts based entirely on your GST return history — no collateral needed. Compare OD limits and interest rates before deciding.
