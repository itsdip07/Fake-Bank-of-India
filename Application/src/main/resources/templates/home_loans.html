A Home Loan is a long-term financing option to help you buy a flat, construct a house, or purchase a plot. It is a secured loan, meaning the property itself acts as the collateral until you repay the full amount.

Loan to Value (LTV) Ratio

The bank funds a percentage of the property cost; the rest is your "Down Payment".

Loan Amount Bank Funding (Max) Your Down Payment
Up to ₹30 Lakhs 90% 10%
₹30L to ₹75 Lakhs 80% 20%
Above ₹75 Lakhs 75% 25%

Processing Fees

  • Standard Fee: 0.35% to 0.50% of the Loan Amount (Max capped at ₹10,000 + GST).
  • Legal & Technical: Actual charges paid to lawyers and valuers for property verification (Approx ₹3,000 - ₹5,000).

Buying a home is not just an emotional decision but a smart financial one. The Government of India provides massive tax incentives to encourage home ownership.

Tax Benefits Explained

Section 24(b)

Deduction on Interest Paid up to ₹2 Lakhs per financial year for a self-occupied property.

Section 80C

Deduction on Principal Repayment up to ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year.

Floating vs Fixed Rate

Most home loans today are "Floating," linked to the Repo Rate.

Feature Floating Rate (RLLR) Fixed Rate
Interest Rate Changes with RBI Repo Rate. Remains constant for a period.
Cost Usually Lower (Current ~8.5%). Usually Higher (Current ~10-11%).
Pre-payment Penalty ZERO (For Individuals). Usually 2% to 4%.

Documents Required

  • KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID.
  • Income: Salary Slips (3 months), Form 16 (2 years), Bank Statements (6 months).
  • Property Papers: Sale Agreement, Title Deed, Approved Plan, Occupancy Certificate.
