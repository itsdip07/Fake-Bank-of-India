The True Cost of Credit

The rewards are real — but so are the penalties. Know these charges before you swipe.

Interest Rate (APR)
Credit card interest in India typically ranges from 36% – 45% per annum (3–3.75% per month). Interest is charged from the transaction date — not the due date — if you carry a balance. Always pay the full outstanding, not just the minimum due, to avoid this compounding trap.
Late Payment Fee & Minimum Due Trap
Missing the payment due date triggers a late payment fee of ₹500 – ₹1,300 (slab-based on outstanding). Paying only the Minimum Amount Due (5% of bill) does NOT stop interest — it simply avoids the late fee while interest accrues on the full balance, creating a debt spiral.
Cash Advance Charges
Withdrawing cash from an ATM using your credit card attracts a cash advance fee of 2.5% (min ₹500) plus interest at the full APR from day one — there is no interest-free period on cash advances. Avoid this unless absolutely necessary.
Foreign Transaction Markup
International transactions are charged a cross-currency markup of 1.5% – 3.5% over the base exchange rate, plus 18% GST on the markup amount. Premium travel cards offer reduced markups of 1–1.5%. Always compare this rate before choosing your overseas spending card.
Over-Limit & Balance Transfer Fees
Exceeding your credit limit incurs an over-limit fee of 2.5% of the excess amount. Balance transfers from other cards attract a processing fee of 1–3% of the transferred amount, though promotional 0% BT offers often waive this for the first 6–12 months.

How It Works

The Credit Card Billing Cycle

Understanding your billing cycle is the single most important thing you can do to use a credit card profitably — and avoid paying a single rupee in interest.

Statement Date
Your bill is generated. All transactions of the past 30 days are consolidated into a statement
Bill Arrives
Total due, minimum due, and payment deadline are shown. You have 15–20 days to pay
Pay Full Balance
Pay the total due before the deadline. Zero interest charged — you kept the free credit window
Cycle Resets
Your full credit limit is restored and a new 30-day interest-free window begins automatically
Decision Guide
How to Choose the Right Card

Apply for the wrong card and you'll leave lakhs of rewards on the table. These three questions narrow it down.

01
Where Do You Spend the Most?

Online shopping → co-branded or cashback card. Travel & dining → Platinum or Travel Elite. Groceries & fuel → general rewards. Match the card's bonus categories to your top spend buckets.

02
Will You Pay in Full Every Month?

If yes — maximise rewards. If sometimes no — choose a low-interest card first, not a high-rewards one. A 40% APR will erase 12 months of points in a single unpaid billing cycle.

03
Does the Annual Fee Justify Itself?

A ₹5,000 annual fee card makes sense only if you extract more than ₹5,000 in value from its benefits. Calculate your break-even spend using the card's reward rate before applying.
