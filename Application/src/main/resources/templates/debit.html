Fee Schedule
Charges to Know About

The card is free — but certain transactions carry small fees. Here's the complete picture.

ATM Withdrawal Charges
You get 5 free transactions per month at Fake Bank of India ATMs and 3 free at other bank ATMs (metro cities). Beyond the limit, charges of ₹20 per transaction apply. International ATM withdrawals carry a flat fee plus a 3.5% foreign exchange markup.
Card Replacement Fee
Lost or damaged cards can be replaced for a fee of ₹200 – ₹500 depending on your card tier. The first replacement is free for Platinum and World card holders. Upgraded cards (chip+PIN to contactless) are issued free of charge.
Foreign Currency Mark-Up
When you transact in a foreign currency, a cross-currency conversion charge of 3.5% is levied over the Mastercard/Visa base rate. Classic and Platinum cards carry this fee; World Signature holders enjoy a reduced 1.5% markup. Always check the DCC (Dynamic Currency Conversion) trap at overseas terminals.
Fraud / Chargeback Process
Report an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days to get zero-liability protection under RBI guidelines. The chargeback process can take 45–90 days to resolve. Enabling transaction SMS alerts and e-mail notifications significantly speeds up fraud detection.

Decision Guide
How to Pick the Right Variant

One size doesn't fit all. These factors will help you choose the perfect card tier.

01
Your Monthly Spend Pattern

If you spend ₹50,000+ per month across groceries, fuel, and dining, a Platinum or World card pays for itself through cashback alone. Classic cards suit light spenders.

02
Travel Frequency

Frequent flyers should prioritise cards with lounge access and lower forex markup. World Signature cards cut the forex charge to 1.5% — a big saving on overseas trips.

03
Online vs. Offline Usage

Online-first shoppers benefit most from a Virtual Debit Card — no physical card to lose, with dedicated limits for e-commerce and instant issuance via the app.
